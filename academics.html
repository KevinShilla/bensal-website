Academics

From a child's first day in day care to their Standard VII exams, everything at Bensal is taught in English, by qualified teachers, following the Tanzanian national curriculum.

From 2 years

Day Care

Our day care takes children from the age of two. For many of them it is their first time away from home, so the focus is on helping them feel safe and settled.

The day is built around play, songs, stories and simple routines. Children start hearing and using English naturally, long before any formal lessons begin.

From 3 years

Pre-Primary

Pre-primary prepares children for Standard I. They learn letters and sounds, begin reading and writing, work with numbers, and get plenty of practice speaking and listening in English.

By the time they move up, children are used to the classroom, comfortable with their teachers, and ready for primary school work.

Standard I – VII

Primary School

Our primary school follows the Tanzanian national curriculum, with English as the language of teaching. Kiswahili is taught as a full subject, and our pupils do very well in both languages.

Pupils sit two national exams with us: the Standard Four National Assessment (SFNA) and the Primary School Leaving Examination (PSLE) at the end of Standard VII. We also use regional and external mock exams through the year so pupils are well practised before the real thing.

Subjects examined at PSLE

These are the six subjects our Standard VII pupils sit in the national exam.

English

The language of every lesson at Bensal.

Kiswahili

Taught as a full subject from the early years.

Mathematics

Number, problem solving and everyday maths.

Science & Technology

How the world works, through practical learning.

Social Studies & Vocational Skills

Tanzania, its people, history and practical skills.

Civics & Ethics

Good citizenship, values and responsibility.
