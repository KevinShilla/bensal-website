Children can join us as toddlers and stay with us until they finish primary school, growing up with the same teachers and friends around them.

Why families choose us

What makes Bensal different

What parents want from us is quality service and a warm welcome. Here is what sets Bensal apart from other schools nearby.

Affordable fees

A good private education at fees families can manage. Fees aren’t published online, so call the office and we’ll take you through them.

Strong academic foundations

Lessons in English from the early years, and strong NECTA results at Standard IV and VII year after year. Our 2024 class averaged 49.3 out of 50 in English.

School transport

Bensal runs its own school buses. Call the office to ask about routes and pick-up points near you.

A safe, caring school

A safe, well-kept campus where teachers and staff look after every child, from day care all the way up to Standard VII.

Every child matters

Discipline, care and attention for every pupil, whether they are top of the class or need a little more help.

Qualified teachers

95% of our teaching staff are university graduates, and they know our pupils well by the time exams come around.

Our story

Started by two educators in 2008

Bensal was founded by Professor Benedict Kayombo and Mrs. Salesia Kayombo.

Today Bensal runs from day care through to Standard VII, and the school keeps growing: our Standard VII class has more than doubled in size since 2019.
