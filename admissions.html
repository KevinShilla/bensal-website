How to apply

Four simple steps

Applications are handled by our admissions office at the school. If you have questions at any point, just call.

Questions about fees?

Our fees are affordable, and they are explained directly by the admissions office rather than online. Call William Mtweve or Furaha Mtweve on 0717 200 904 / 0688 900 066.

  1. Get in touch

    Call the admissions office to ask about places for your child's age or class.

  2. Visit the school

    Come to Tungi to look around, see the classrooms and meet our teachers. The office is open Monday to Friday, 7:30am to 4:00pm.

  3. Fill in the application form

    Collect the form from the school office and hand it back once it's filled in. The office will tell you which documents to bring.

  4. Hear back from us

    The admissions office will contact you to confirm a place, the fees and your child's start date.

Questions

Things parents often ask

Can't find your answer? Call or WhatsApp us and we'll help.

Is Bensal a boarding school?

No. Bensal is a day school. Children come in each morning and go home at the end of the school day.

What language are lessons taught in?

English. Bensal is an English medium school, and Kiswahili is taught as a subject.

Do you accept children of all religions?

Yes. We are a private school and families of all religions are welcome.

How much are the fees?

Fees are not published online. Please contact the admissions office and they will take you through the fees for your child's class.

Can my child join in the middle of primary school?

Yes. We accept new pupils in all classes, and places are currently available in Standard I to Standard VI. Call the admissions office to confirm a place for your child.

When is enrolment?

Enrolment is open all year round, so your child can join at any time of the year.

How old does my child need to be?

Children need to be at least 3 years old to join the school (pre-primary and primary). Our day care takes younger children from the age of two.

Do you have school transport?

Yes. Bensal runs its own school buses. Call the office to ask about routes and pick-up points.

Is Bensal a registered school?

Yes. Bensal is registered by the Ministry of Education (registration no. MG.01/7/EA.016 / MG.01/7/016), and is a registered NECTA examination centre (PS1104081), so pupils sit their Standard IV and Standard VII national exams at the school.
