About Bensal

A family-founded school in Tungi, Morogoro, teaching children from day care to Standard VII since 2008.

Our story

How Bensal began

Bensal was started in 2008 by Professor Benedict Kayombo and Professor Salesia Kayombo. The idea was a school in Morogoro where children learn in English from the very start, are taught by well-qualified teachers, and are expected to do well.

Their names live on in the school's own name. See how ↓

Today Bensal includes a day care for children from two years old, a pre-primary section, and a full primary school from Standard I to Standard VII. The school is registered with NECTA as an examination centre, and our pupils sit the national Standard IV and Standard VII exams here at Bensal.

The school has grown steadily. In 2019, 41 pupils sat the Standard VII exam. In 2025 it was 87.

Where the name comes from

Ben + Sal = Bensal

The school is named after its two founders. Take the first three letters of each of their names, put them together, and you get Bensal.

What matters to us

How we run the school

High expectations

We expect every child to do well, and we put in the work to help them get there. Our national exam results show what that looks like in practice.

Good teachers

95% of our teachers hold a university degree. We believe a well-trained teacher is the most important thing a school can give a child.

Open to all

Bensal is a private school for families of every religion. Children of different faiths and backgrounds learn and play together here every day.
