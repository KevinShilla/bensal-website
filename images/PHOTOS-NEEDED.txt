ADDING MORE SLIDESHOW PHOTOS
  Copy one of the <figure class="slide"> lines in index.html and change
  the file name, description and caption. It picks up the new slide
  automatically and keeps changing every 5 seconds.
  Photos of any shape work: the whole photo is always shown, so nobody
  at the edge of a group photo gets cut off.

NO CROPPING IN THE GALLERY
  The School Life gallery keeps every photo at its own shape. Add
  class="photo span-all" to a wide group photo to show it across the
  full width, like pupils-lineup.jpg.

ORGANISATION CHART
  The "How the school is organised" chart on the About page is built in
  the page itself (about.html, section id="leadership"), not an image.
  To change a name or title, edit the text there.

Tip: resize photos to about 1600px wide before adding them so pages load fast.
