ADDING MORE SLIDESHOW PHOTOS
  Copy one of the <figure class="slide"> lines in index.html and change
  the file name, description and caption. It picks up the new slide
  automatically and keeps changing every 5 seconds.

Tip: resize photos to about 1600px wide before adding them so pages load fast.
